 [[0. AWS-SAA-Training Overview]] 

Services that have servers behind them, and some other comapny is responsible for running and maintaining the service and servers, are serverless services (i.e. EFS, CloudFront, Route53, Lambda)

**Serverless Computing**- Running your applications and code without having to worry about the servers that are running your applications and code.
