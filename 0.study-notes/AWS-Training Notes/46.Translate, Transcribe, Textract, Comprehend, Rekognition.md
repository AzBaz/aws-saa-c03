 [[0. AWS-SAA-Training Overview]]

## Amazon Translate
Neural machine **translation** service. Converts text from one language to another (75  languages).
- Uses deep learning for natural, context-aware translations.
- Supports **real-time** and **batch** translation.
- **Auto-detects source language**.
- Integrates with Comprehend, Transcribe, Polly to build pipelines (e.g., transcribe speech, then translate).

**Scenario cue**: "translate text between languages" → Translate.

---

## Amazon Transcribe 
**Speech-to-text** (automatic speech recognition). Converts audio/video into text.
- Supports **real-time streaming** and **batch** transcription.
- **Automatic PII redaction** (removes sensitive data from transcripts).
- **Speaker diarization** (identifies who spoke).
- **Custom vocabulary** for domain-specific terms.
- **Transcribe Medical**, HIPAA-eligible variant for clinical speech.

**Scenario cue**: "convert audio/call recordings to text" → Transcribe.

---
## Amazon Textract
Extracts **text, forms, and tables from documents** (scanned images, PDFs). Goes beyond basic OCR.
- Understands **structure**: key-value pairs (forms), table cells, not just raw characters.
- **Analyze Document** for forms/tables; **Analyze Expense** for invoices/receipts; **Analyze ID** for identity documents.
- Output feeds into downstream processing, databases, or Comprehend.

**Scenario cue**: "extract data from scanned forms/invoices/PDFs" → Textract.

---

## Amazon Comprehend
 Uncover valuable insights from text in documents, customer support tickets. product reviews, emails, social media feeds, more. 
**Natural Language Processing (NLP)**. Finds insights and relationships in **text**.
- Detects: **sentiment**, **entities** (names, places, dates), **key phrases**, **language**, **PII**, **topics**.
- **Comprehend Medical**, extracts medical info (conditions, meds) from clinical text; HIPAA-eligible.
- Supports **custom classification** and **custom entity recognition**.

**Scenario cue**: "analyze text for sentiment / extract meaning / find entities" → Comprehend.

---

## Amazon Rekognition
**Image and video analysis** using deep learning.
- Detects **objects, scenes, faces, text in images**, and **inappropriate/unsafe content** (content moderation).
- **Facial analysis and comparison** (identity verification, face matching).
- **Celebrity recognition**, **PPE detection**, **face search** against a collection.
- Works on **images and streaming/stored video**.

**Scenario cue**: "analyze images or video / detect faces / moderate content" → Rekognition.

---

**Confusion pairs (exam traps)**
- **Textract vs Rekognition text**: Textract = structured document data (forms/tables). Rekognition = text detected _in images/scenes_ (like a sign or label). "Invoice/form" → Textract.
- **Transcribe vs Comprehend**: Transcribe = audio _to_ text. Comprehend = _analyze_ text meaning. A full pipeline often chains them.
- **Translate vs Comprehend**: Translate = change language. Comprehend = understand content (including detecting language, but not translating).
- **Comprehend Medical vs Transcribe Medical**: Comprehend Medical extracts info from clinical _text_; Transcribe Medical converts clinical _speech_ to text.

**Common pipeline pattern** (shows up in scenarios)
- Audio → **Transcribe** → text → **Comprehend** (insights) or **Translate** (other language) → **Polly** (back to speech).
