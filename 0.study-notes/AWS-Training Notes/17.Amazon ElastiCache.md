Problem: Accessing information from DB might be too slow even with read replicas. 
Solution: **Caching**
	Store and retrieve data from fast in-memory data stores instead of slow hard disks

Great for:
	Read-heavy applications where the same results are returned over and over again
		i.e. Social Media, Gamin, Media Sharing, Leader Boards
	Catalogs of items
	Session information
	Compute-intensive apps recommendation engine.
